# How to add a product in Marthington BMS

## 1) Open the products page
- Log in to the BMS dashboard.
- In the left menu, click Products.
- Make sure you are on the product list page.

## 2) Open the Add Product form
- Click the Add product button or the product creation panel.
- The form will appear with fields for:
  - Product name
  - Cost price
  - Selling price
  - Markup (%)
  - Stock quantity

## 3) Fill in the product details
Enter the following:
- Product name: e.g. "Premium Notebook"
- Cost Price: the amount you paid for one unit
- Selling Price: the amount customers will pay
- Markup (%): if you enter cost price and markup, the selling price will be suggested automatically
- Stock: how many items are available

Tip:
- If you enter a selling price directly, the markup is calculated automatically.
- If you change the markup, the selling price updates automatically.

## 4) Save the product
- Review the details.
- Click Create product.
- The item will appear in your product list and be ready for stock and sales activity.

## 5) Check the saved product
- Go back to the Products page.
- Confirm the new product appears in the list.
- You can now edit stock, pricing, or details if needed.

## Optional quick reminder
- Keep cost and selling prices realistic.
- Avoid leaving stock blank.
- Use a consistent product naming format for easier search later.
